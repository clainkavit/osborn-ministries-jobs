**Resolved 2026-09-09.** [project-brief.md](../project-brief.md) originally scoped a fast single-employer launch (fill three roles for one waiting businessman). [stage-1-project-definition.md](../stage-1-project-definition.md) scoped a much larger general-purpose professional network. The two were unreconciled as of the brief and Stage 1's initial write-up.

The pastor has since confirmed the full network vision. [stage-2-prd.md](../stage-2-prd.md) is the approved product direction, not the brief's three-role slice as an end state — that slice was the proof-of-concept case that started this, and is separately confirmed filled/handled.

The related warranty question (does "verified" mean the church guarantees a member's conduct) is also resolved, in two steps. PRD sections 19-21 first answered it at the product level: verification badges, scoped to "information reviewed" rather than a conduct guarantee. [stage-3-ux.md](../stage-3-ux.md) then resolved it at the copy level, since the badge concept alone wasn't enough — its closing section sets the exact badge labels: **"✓ Membership confirmed"** and **"✓ Credentials reviewed"**, replacing the originally-drafted "Church Member Verified" / "Verified Professional," because employers scan badges and rarely open hover text, so the honest version of the claim has to be the label itself, not text hidden behind a hover.

**Why this file still exists (not deleted):** the resolution and its reasoning are worth keeping visible so a later screen or feature doesn't quietly reintroduce a "Church Verified = vouched for" badge.

**How to apply:** Stage 4 (wireframes) should use the two labels above verbatim, everywhere a verification badge appears. The legal/compliance items (employment-agency status, PDPA) are tracked separately, see [[compliance-deferred-to-post-launch]] — they are no longer gated before Phase 1, that guidance changed on 2026-09-09.
