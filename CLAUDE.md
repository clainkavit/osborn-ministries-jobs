## Two things to hold onto

**The product is the vouching, not the database.** A searchable CV store is free and solved. Employers are coming because members of this congregation are known. Check design decisions against that, not against what a job board usually has.

**Verification means "information reviewed," not "conduct guaranteed."** The PRD's Member Verified / Profession Verified badges are the resolution to the earlier warranty question, on one condition: badge copy shown to employers must say what was checked (membership confirmed, credentials reviewed), not imply an endorsement of future behavior. See PRD section 19's implementation note. Get this wrong in the UI and the platform ends up making the personal guarantee the pastor gave verbally to one businessman into a standing product claim, which is exactly what was meant to be avoided.

## Legal and data posture

This project collects personal data (CVs, ages, education, contact details, certifications, documents) and sits near employment-agency regulation in Tanzania. **Deliberately deferred, not unresolved by oversight:** Champion decided (2026-09-09) that PDPA registration and the employment-agency question get addressed once the system is confirmed running, not as a pre-build or pre-launch gate. See [context/memory/compliance_deferred_to_post_launch.md](context/memory/compliance_deferred_to_post_launch.md) — don't re-raise these as blockers; they're a post-launch checkpoint.

**No longer deferred:** the technology stack (see [stage-19-technical-architecture.md](context/stage-19-technical-architecture.md) — Next.js/TypeScript, Supabase/PostgreSQL, Vercel), the opportunity-closing rule, the interview-contact mechanism, reverification-on-edit, and church/branch architecture are all decided as of 2026-09-10, following an external review of [COMBINED-SPEC.md](COMBINED-SPEC.md). See each relevant stage document (7, 8, 15, 19, and the PRD's closing section) rather than assuming these are still open.

**What IS still open — real gaps with no document that can resolve them, only Champion's or Church Admins' decision:**
- **Verification criteria.** Church Admins are confirmed as who verifies, but nobody has defined the actual standard they apply (length of membership, attendance, a reference). Blocks the verification workflow meaning anything in practice. **This is the single most-repeated open item across every stage document — resolve this first, before anything else on this list.**
- Does an opportunity auto-transition to Filled when headcount is reached (Stage 7).
- Does "Reviewed" (the application status, not verification) notify the member (Stage 6).
- Does publishing a new opportunity need any admin approval step (Stage 6).
- Suspension/inactivity thresholds for members (Stage 7) — schema should reserve room, workflow not designed yet.

Assumptions are the enemy. If the answer is not in the PRD, a stage document, or memory, ask Champion.
